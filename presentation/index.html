The challenge: make health evidence usable under constraints

Long-duration missions make it important to notice changes while keeping uncertainty and data quality visible.

THE DESIGN PROBLEM

Health indicators are only useful when their source, completeness and limits are understood.

  • NASA research studies contain different data types and collection designs.
  • A missing measurement must remain missing — not be silently invented.
  • A disconnected environment requires core functions to work locally.

02 · SYSTEM DESIGN
03 / 07

Three modes, three clearly separated evidence types

ASTRA is designed as one workspace without blending historical NASA measurements with fictional or user-entered signals.

NASA RESEARCH MODE

Retrospective biomarker exploration

Verified OSD-575 transformed serum assay and ISA sample map.

  • Source hash check
  • 28/28 sample-to-metadata join
  • Nine-analyte normalization audit
DEMO & MANUAL MODE

Local indicator review

User-entered or simulated HR, SpO₂, temperature and sleep.

  • Input validation
  • Reference-snapshot comparison
  • Wellbeing self-report context
MISSION REPLAY

Synthetic 30-day scenario playback

Deterministic fictional telemetry and browser-local experimental MLP.

  • Seven scenarios
  • Rolling seven-day window
  • CSV export and data-quality gate
EVIDENCE TYPE IS LABELLED AT SOURCE AND REMAINS SEPARATE THROUGHOUT THE APP

04 · UNCERTAINTY HANDLING
05 / 07

A deliberate abstention gate prevents unsupported inference

Sensor dropout is a data-quality condition — not a class the MLP is trained to recognize.

7-DAY WINDOW

Recent daily readings

QUALITY GATE

Completeness checks

MLP v2

Only when gate passes

When does ASTRA abstain?

  • Fewer than 5 of 7 daily values for any vital-sign channel.
  • Fewer than 5 of 7 fatigue or stress self-reports.
  • More than 25% of vital-sign cells missing in the rolling window.
EXPECTED OUTPUT
MODEL ABSTAINED

Score: — · Missing values stay missing.

MLP v2 was trained only on generated synthetic windows. Its 0–1 score is an uncalibrated synthetic-pattern similarity score — not a probability or medical risk.

06 · IMPACT & LIMITATIONS
07 / 07

A trustworthy prototype — not a claim of clinical readiness

ASTRA demonstrates how evidence provenance, data quality and graceful abstention can be built into a compact offline-first workflow.

WHAT ASTRA DELIVERS
  • A reproducible workspace for NASA-derived retrospective biomarker exploration.
  • Separate synthetic/manual workflows for vital-sign interface demonstrations.
  • A local model screen that refuses to infer when the seven-day evidence window is incomplete.
  • Offline access to the cached application, source file and local model.
WHAT ASTRA DOES NOT CLAIM
  • No connected wearable or live spacecraft telemetry.
  • No clinical validation or real-astronaut model evaluation.
  • No diagnosis, event prediction, treatment advice or medical risk probability.
  • OSD-575 remains a small retrospective biospecimen dataset.
