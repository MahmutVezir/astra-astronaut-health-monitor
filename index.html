How comparison works

Each available reading is checked against the demo crew member's own reference band. A value outside that band is marked “Review”. Missing values are marked “No data”. The system never fills gaps or turns this check into a diagnosis.

WHY IT MATTERS

Health is more than a vital sign.

Long-duration missions also create behavioral and operational pressures. A brief self-report can help crew members notice patterns and add context to a trend.

Self-reports are not psychological diagnoses. They are optional context shared by the crew member.
