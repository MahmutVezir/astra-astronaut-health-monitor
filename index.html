PROVENANCE FIRST

Public catalogue visibility is not the same as file-level validation. Every candidate must pass exact-file identification, download, byte preservation, SHA-256, schema review and a reproducible analysis before any measurements appear in ASTRA.

What ASTRA does — and does not — claim

  • All dashboard readings and crew entries in this demonstration are simulated or manually entered by the user.
  • OSD-575 is referenced as a real NASA open research study; its biospecimen assay data are not used to fabricate vital-sign readings.
  • Personal reference bands are illustrative demo settings, not NASA standards or medical thresholds.
  • ASTRA does not diagnose, predict a medical event, prescribe treatment, or replace a flight surgeon or mission medical protocol.
  • No wearable, spacecraft telemetry, or remote medical connection is implemented.
  • Local browser storage is not an encrypted medical-record system. Do not enter real, identifiable, or sensitive health information.

How the AI works and where it stops

ASTRA uses a locally executed, interpretable decision tree. It evaluates eight features: four signal changes, missing-data rate, stale-data rate, flatline rate, and wellbeing self-report flags. The model was trained and holdout-tested only on simulator-generated synthetic examples. A model-support value describes the class share in the reached training leaf; it is not a clinical probability.

The system abstains when a window is too short, signal coverage is inadequate, a feature is outside the supported envelope, or leaf support is below 65%. Model output is separate from fixed reference-band and personal-reference checks. It does not diagnose, predict a medical event, recommend treatment, or establish flight readiness.
