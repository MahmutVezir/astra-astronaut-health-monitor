What ASTRA does — and does not — claim

  • All dashboard readings and crew entries in this demonstration are simulated or manually entered by the user.
  • OSD-575 is referenced as a real NASA open research study; its biospecimen assay data are not used to fabricate vital-sign readings.
  • Personal reference bands are illustrative demo settings, not NASA standards or medical thresholds.
  • ASTRA does not diagnose, predict a medical event, prescribe treatment, or replace a flight surgeon or mission medical protocol.
  • No wearable, spacecraft telemetry, or remote medical connection is implemented.
  • Local browser storage is not an encrypted medical-record system. Do not enter real, identifiable, or sensitive health information.
